# Professional Portfolio Website

## Project Overview

The Professional Portfolio Website is a personal project I created to showcase my professional background, leadership experience, technology initiatives, and ongoing projects.

Using AI-assisted development, I planned, designed, and launched a website that brings together my professional experience and technical interests in a centralized, accessible format.

The project has provided hands-on experience with website design, hosting, deployment, version control, analytics, and ongoing maintenance while expanding my understanding of web technologies.

## Project Planning & Development

I approached the project with an emphasis on creating a professional, user-friendly website that could be easily maintained and expanded as my experience and projects evolve.

Key areas of focus included:

- **Planning & Requirements:** Defining the website's purpose, content, organization, and overall design.
- **AI-Assisted Development:** Using AI tools to support website creation, troubleshooting, and refinement while directing the overall project.
- **Deployment & Version Control:** Learning how to publish, manage, and update a live website using GitHub and web hosting services.
- **Website Analytics:** Incorporating analytics to better understand website traffic, visitor engagement, and how the site is being accessed.
- **Digital Business Card:** Creating a digital business card with a QR code linking directly to my professional portfolio, providing a convenient way to share my contact information, professional background, and projects during networking opportunities.
- **Testing & Validation:** Reviewing layout, navigation, responsiveness, and functionality to ensure a consistent user experience.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Making incremental updates based on changing requirements, usability, and presentation.

## Project Outcomes

The result is a live professional portfolio that provides a centralized location for my career background, qualifications, and technology projects, supported by website analytics to monitor engagement and a digital business card for convenient sharing and professional networking.

The website continues to evolve as I expand my technical knowledge and develop additional projects.

This experience has reinforced the importance of defining clear objectives, managing changes, validating results, and maintaining a product beyond its initial launch.
