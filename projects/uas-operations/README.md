# UAS Operations Management Platform

## Project Overview

The UAS Operations Management Platform is an ongoing personal project focused on developing a centralized system to support the management of public safety drone programs.

The idea came from my experience overseeing UAS operations and recognizing opportunities to improve how agencies manage personnel, training, certifications, flight records, equipment, and operational information across multiple systems.

Drawing on my operational experience, I've approached the project with an emphasis on usability, systems integration, security, scalability, and reusability. A key consideration has been designing a platform that can support the needs of an individual agency while remaining adaptable to different organizations and future requirements.

## Project Planning & AI-Assisted Development

I've approached this project through a phased development process, using AI-assisted development to translate operational requirements into application features and establish the foundation for a larger UAS management platform.

My role has focused on identifying operational needs, defining requirements, establishing priorities, directing development, evaluating technical approaches, and validating results. The project has also provided an opportunity to expand my technical understanding while applying project and program management principles.

Key areas of focus include:

- **Requirements & Planning:** Identifying challenges in managing UAS programs and translating operational needs into application requirements.
- **Milestone Management:** Organizing development into manageable phases, prioritizing foundational capabilities, and reviewing progress before expanding functionality.
- **AI-Assisted Development:** Using AI tools to support application design, implementation, troubleshooting, and refinement while maintaining oversight of project direction.
- **Systems Integration:** Evaluating opportunities to connect aviation weather information, flight records, personnel management, and other operational systems.
- **Technical Decision-Making:** Considering architectural approaches, database requirements, integration dependencies, and long-term maintainability.
- **Testing & Validation:** Using automated testing and manual reviews to evaluate functionality, identify issues, and verify intended behavior.
- **Change Management:** Reviewing proposed changes, assessing their impact, and maintaining control over implementation and version history.

## Technical Architecture & Security

Through this project, I've been gaining practical experience with the technical considerations involved in planning a platform that brings together multiple operational functions and external data sources.

Key areas of focus include:

- **Application Architecture:** Understanding how application components, backend services, and databases work together.
- **APIs & Systems Integration:** Exploring how external services can exchange information with a centralized application, including aviation weather data and potential integrations with UAS management systems.
- **Data Management:** Considering how personnel records, certifications, flight information, and equipment data can be organized and maintained.
- **Security & Data Protection:** Evaluating authentication, access controls, secure application design, and CJIS security requirements where applicable to public safety operations.
- **Reliability & Maintainability:** Considering data validation, error handling, automated testing, and the long-term support of integrated systems.
- **Scalability & Reusability:** Exploring approaches that could allow the platform to support multiple agencies, different aircraft systems, and evolving operational requirements.

Security, data protection, and reliable system integration remain important considerations throughout development, particularly when working with potentially sensitive operational information.

## Project Direction

The initial development effort has focused on aviation weather functionality and establishing the technical foundation for a broader UAS operations management platform.

Planned capabilities include centralized personnel and certification tracking, flight record management, equipment and maintenance tracking, operational planning, and integration with external systems.

The long-term objective is to develop a flexible platform that helps agencies manage UAS programs more efficiently while reducing the need to maintain information across disconnected systems.
