## About Me

I am a Police Lieutenant with a background in operational leadership, criminal investigations, and public safety technology. I am currently assigned to patrol operations, where I oversee our Field Training Program and provide administrative oversight of our Real-Time Intelligence Center (RTIC), Automated License Plate Recognition (ALPR), and Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS) programs.

Throughout my career, I have helped lead the planning, implementation, and management of technology initiatives, including Axon Fusus, ALPR systems, UAS program, camera network expansion, and covert investigative technologies. These projects have involved leading teams, coordinating personnel and resources, collaborating with vendors and stakeholders, navigating operational challenges, and overseeing the implementation and integration of new technologies into daily operations.

My interest in technology extends beyond my professional responsibilities. Through self-directed learning and AI-assisted development, I have been building applications that address practical operational needs while expanding my understanding of software architecture, APIs, databases, system integration, and secure application design, with an emphasis on CJIS security requirements and data protection.
